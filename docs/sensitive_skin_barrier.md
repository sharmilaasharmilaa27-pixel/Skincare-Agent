# Sensitive Skin and Barrier Repair

## Introduction

Sensitive skin is characterized by reactivity, redness, stinging, burning, and itching in response to products or environmental factors. A compromised skin barrier is often the underlying cause, allowing irritants to penetrate and moisture to escape. Barrier repair focuses on restoring the lipid matrix and reducing inflammation.

## Understanding Sensitive Skin

### Causes

Sensitive skin can be genetic (inherent) or acquired. Genetic sensitivity often runs in families and involves a thinner stratum corneum and reduced lipid production. Acquired sensitivity results from over-exfoliation, harsh products, environmental damage, or medical conditions like eczema and rosacea.

### Compromised Barrier Signs

A compromised barrier manifests as persistent redness, tightness, stinging after product application, flaking, and increased sensitivity to previously tolerated products. The skin may also react to environmental triggers like wind, cold, and pollution.

## Barrier Structure and Function

The skin barrier consists of corneocytes (dead skin cells) held together by a lipid matrix of ceramides, cholesterol, and fatty acids. This "mortar and brick" structure prevents water loss and blocks external irritants. When the lipid matrix is depleted, the barrier becomes permeable and vulnerable.

## Key Barrier-Repair Ingredients

### Ceramides

Ceramides are lipids that make up approximately 50% of the stratum corneum lipids. They are essential for barrier integrity and preventing transepidermal water loss. Topical ceramides replenish the lipid matrix, restore barrier function, and reduce sensitivity. Ceramide-rich moisturizers are foundational for sensitive and compromised skin.

### Centella Asiatica (Cica)

Centella asiatica contains asiaticoside, madecassoside, and asiatic acid that promote wound healing, reduce inflammation, and stimulate collagen production. It is highly effective for calming irritated skin, reducing redness, and repairing damaged barriers. It is gentle enough for even the most reactive skin.

### Niacinamide

Niacinamide strengthens the skin barrier by promoting ceramide synthesis, reduces inflammation, and regulates sebum production. It is well-tolerated by sensitive skin and helps restore barrier function over time.

### Panthenol (Vitamin B5)

Panthenol is a provitamin that converts to vitamin B5 in the skin. It deeply hydrates, reduces inflammation, promotes wound healing, and soothes irritation. It is a staple ingredient in barrier-repair formulations.

### Fatty Acids and Cholesterol

Linoleic acid, oleic acid, cholesterol, and ceramides in specific ratios (ideally 1:1:1) restore the natural lipid matrix of the barrier. Products formulated with these three lipid classes in balanced proportions are most effective for barrier repair.

## Ingredients to Avoid with Sensitive Skin

- **Fragrance:** Synthetic and essential fragrances are common irritants and allergens.
- **Alcohol (denat./ethanol):** Drying and disruptive to the barrier at high concentrations.
- **Strong acids (AHA/BHA):** Can over-exfoliate and damage the already-compromised barrier.
- **Physical exfoliants:** Scrubs and brushes can cause micro-tears in sensitive skin.
- **Retinoids:** Can cause significant irritation on sensitive skin until tolerance builds.

## Rosacea and Barrier Health

Rosacea is a chronic condition with redness, visible blood vessels, and sometimes papules. Trigger management (heat, spicy food, alcohol, stress) and gentle barrier-repair products are essential. Ingredients like centella asiatica, niacinamide, and azelaic acid help manage rosacea symptoms.

## Sensitive Skin Routine

1. **Cleanse:** Use a gentle, fragrance-free, non-foaming cleanser.
2. **Treat:** Apply centella asiatica or niacinamide serum.
3. **Moisturize:** Use a ceramide-rich, fragrance-free moisturizer.
4. **Protect:** Use a mineral (zinc oxide/titanium dioxide) sunscreen.
5. **Introduce products one at a time** to identify triggers.

## Key Takeaways

- Ceramides, centella asiatica, and niacinamide are core barrier-repair ingredients.
- Avoid fragrance, alcohol, and harsh acids on sensitive skin.
- Mineral sunscreens are gentler than chemical alternatives.
- Introduce products slowly to identify triggers.
- A healthy barrier is the foundation of calm, resilient skin.
